Hi, I'm Clive Curry, a drone photographer and student at Wake Forest University. I've been flying for about six years, and what started as a hobby has turned into something I take seriously: finding the view of a place that you just can't get from the ground.

This portfolio is a collection of my favorite work from across the U.S. and abroad, from Hawaii and the Oregon coast to Grand Cayman and the Swiss Alps. The shots range from lightning storms and mountain ranges to coastlines and city skylines, and each one is a moment I'm glad I got in the air for.

A good aerial photo takes a lot more than just putting a drone up. It takes planning, patience, keeping an eye on the weather and light, and knowing how to frame a scene from hundreds of feet up. I'm always working to get better, and this site is a record of that progress.

Thanks for stopping by. Free to reach out at clivecurry@outlook.com.
